NEED TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

How do I order?+

Add a product to your bag, open checkout, enter your delivery details and submit the order request.

How do I pay?+

The current storefront records the order as pending. Razorpay can be enabled once the payment order creation and server-side verification are deployed in the DEWIFY backend.

How does delivery work?+

Delivery coverage and timing depend on the destination and fulfilment route. Avoid publishing a fixed delivery promise until that route is confirmed.

Need help with an order?+

Use the DEWIFY support link in the footer and keep your DEWIFY order ID ready.

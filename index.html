05 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do digital products get delivered?+

After a successful Paddle checkout, DEWIFY can show the product access link on this page. Paddle also provides the customer's transaction receipt.

Do I need a PayPal account to buy?+

No. Paddle displays the payment methods available for the customer's location and checkout session. The buyer chooses from the methods presented by Paddle.

Are physical products sold here?+

Not right now. This version of DEWIFY is intentionally focused on digital products only.

Need support?+

Email dewifystores@gmail.com with the product name and your Paddle transaction ID.

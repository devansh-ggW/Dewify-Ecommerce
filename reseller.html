02 / LICENSE FIRST

Do this before
you list anything.

This is the bit that keeps a reseller model clean. A product being downloadable or editable does not automatically mean every form of redistribution is allowed.

Open the included license

Check exactly what the specific pack allows: resale, modification, client use, branding, redistribution, or other restrictions.

Replace placeholders

Remove fictional names, sample contact details and other demo content before publishing a finished template or deliverable.

Audit third-party assets

Some files may contain third-party fonts, images, icons or other components with separate terms.

Keep your promise accurate

Describe the rights you actually have. Do not advertise a pack as unlimited resale inventory unless its license says that.

DEWIFY product pages use license-first language for this reason. The included license is the source of truth for the specific pack you purchase.

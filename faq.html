What is DEWIFY?

A digital wholesale-style storefront for ready-made digital inventory, including creator packs, templates and learning products.

Can every product be resold?

No blanket promise. Resale and redistribution rights depend on the license included with the specific product.

Can I rebrand the files?

Only where the product's license permits modification or branding. Check the included terms before publishing.

How do I get the files?

Current digital products use Paddle for checkout and unlock a ZIP delivery flow after successful payment.

Are these subscriptions?

The current creator packs are set up as one-time digital purchases. Taxes and final checkout totals are shown by Paddle.

Can I use templates for clients?

Some product pages describe client or commercial use, but third-party assets and the individual license can add restrictions.
